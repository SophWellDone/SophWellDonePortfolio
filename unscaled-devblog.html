UNSCALED - A 3D INDIE GAME

Sophie Wheldon
March 29, 2026
Updated on April 12, 2026

UNSCALED is my biggest passion project - a full indie game that I plan to release whenever I feel it's ready! Its a singleplayer, 3D hero-based movement shooter that has used and taught me so many game development related skills. 

As it is such a large project, I post 2 different types of content to showcase my work - devlog entries and deep dives. Devlog entries are videos showcasing progress and other less serious moments with the community for the game, whilst Deep Dives focus on my implementation of specific features / skills such as code, art, shaders or design philosophies!
